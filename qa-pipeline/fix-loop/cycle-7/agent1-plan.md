## Remaining defects, source evidence, and ordered steps

Run the full backend suite after **each** step with `--ignore=tests/test_browser_autofill.py`; compare against the immediately preceding run. Convert only fully flipped strict xfails to plain tests, leaving their assertions intact. If only some parameters flip, split the parametrization. A lost pass is a regression, even when a formerly xfailed case improves.

1. **C4, both directions together — `response_guard.py::_percent_role` and `_apply_outcome_probability`.** `_PROB_BEFORE_RE` only admits copula/approximator words in a 40-character gap, so ordinary `chance ... roughly 30% in your case` or `sambhavna is case mein ... 30%` survives. The leading `_MEASURAND_AFTER_RE` veto incorrectly saves `30% refund chance` and `65% refund chance`: `refund` describes what the claim concerns, while `chance` is the percentage's asserted role. Conversely, `_OUTCOME_LINE_RE` has an optional colon and a `[^:\n]{0,90}` label, making any line-ending rate near an outcome word look like a win probability; it deletes `Interest you can claim on a favourable order: 18%`, normal Hindi/Hinglish rate labels, and two bullets in the existing markdown-rate-list reproducer. **Plan:** classify each percentage by its own clause/label and governing noun phrase, not a proximity window. An explicit probability claim (including a probability noun after a subject matter such as `refund chance`) must beat a generic quantity-word veto; an explicit rate/measurand governing the figure (`interest ...: 18%`, `12% GST`, `100% refund`) must be kept even if an outcome word appears in a subordinate phrase. Tighten/remove the whole-line co-occurrence shortcut and use one verdict for line and inline paths. When grammar is inconclusive, keep the text and record no `outcome_probability` hit or note. Preserve handling of ranges and W1-06 t4's four actual invented percentages, including its U+202F and U+2011 bytes. **Do not** implement the old suggestion “probability noun anywhere earlier plus measurand-after veto”; the cycle-6 report proves both halves independently unsafe. Existing strict xfails: `test_c4_a_rate_at_the_end_of_a_line_survives_an_outcome_word_in_its_label` (6), `test_c4_a_devanagari_rate_survives_when_its_measurand_sits_in_the_label` (2), `test_c4_a_markdown_rate_list_keeps_every_rate_and_earns_no_probability_note` (1), `test_c4_a_win_probability_in_ordinary_prose_is_still_removed` (9), and `test_c4_a_measurand_after_the_number_does_not_rescue_a_win_probability` (7).
